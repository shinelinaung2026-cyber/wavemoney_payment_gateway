How to get a Bot Token:

  1. First create bot from @BotFather
  2. Send /newbot to @BotFather
  3. Type your bot name
  4. Then type your bot username (must end with _bot)
  5. You'll get your bot token - copy it
  6. Paste the token above and click Create Bot
  7. Make the bot admin in your channel/group to get reactions
